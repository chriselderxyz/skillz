# Thumbnail Rules
- Give each thumbnail one focal point.
- Use 5 words or fewer across the title and subtitle.
- Size text to stay readable at 20% scale.
- Keep text and faces clear of the bottom-right corner, 20% of the width by 15% of the height.
- Write package, function, and variable names in the same case as the code, such as `useState`, even in an all-caps title.
- Keep code snippets to 5 short lines or fewer.
