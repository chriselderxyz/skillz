# go-tutorial

## Style
- Playful, clean Go tutorial look with the wizard gopher as the channel's host.
- Low density on a flat background, with roughly 40% of the frame empty.

## Layout
- Put the wizard gopher in the right third, large, and cropped by the bottom edge.
- Run the title across the top, or down the left half when it is one word.
- Fill the left or center with one supporting element: a scene gopher acting out the topic, a code snippet, or a short comparison such as "slog+ vs. zerolog".

## Assets
- Wizard Gopher:
    - Path: `assets/images/go-gopher/gopher - wizard.png`.
    - Description: The channel's host, a Go gopher in a blue wizard hat holding a star wand.
    - Modifications: Change its eyes or pose to react to the topic, such as spiral eyes for a confusing topic.
- Gopher:
    - Path: `assets/images/go-gopher/gopher.png`.
    - Description: The plain Go gopher, used as the base for scene gophers that act out the topic.
    - Modifications: Change its expression, outfit, or props to fit the topic.
