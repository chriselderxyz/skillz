# Structural Signatures
## Cold Opens
### With Temperature

Cold Tub videos state the water temp in the first three lines, often with a bit:

> "And the duck says were sitting at… **temperature**"
> "Annnd we're sitting at **Temperature**, very cozy."
> "We're sittin at… **temp** Fahrenheit, or **temp** Celcius."

### Hook First, Intro Second
The thesis is line one. No preamble, no "in today's video". The self-intro is *delayed* until after the hook has landed:

> "Keep your code DRY is the worst advice I can imagine giving a new programmer."
> *(…four lines of argument…)*
> "Hi, I'm Chris, and this is Cold Tub Tech!"

### Other Examples
- *"Recursion is never faster than regular loop iteration."*
- *"Stop. Putting. Everything. In. A Goroutine."*
- *"Go has wayyyyy too many ways to create a variable."*
