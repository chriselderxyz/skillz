# Local Research Guide Purpose
- Guidelines to follow when looking for local files as a source of information for writing the script.
- Gives guidance around where to look, what types of files to look for, and what to avoid.

# Research Guide Structure
```md
# Search Guidelines
- Where to look.
- What type of files to look for.
- What files to avoid.
# Reading Guidelines
- When and what files to fully read.
# Other Instructions
- Additional instructions for what to do with the files that are found.
```
