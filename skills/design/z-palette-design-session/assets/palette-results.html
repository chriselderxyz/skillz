Token mapping schemes

Each scheme maps every token to a ramp step for light and dark mode. Every scheme follows Radix's step semantics (1–2 backgrounds, 3–5 component fills, 6–8 borders, 9–10 solids, 11–12 text) and keeps background on step 1 so steps 2–3 stay free for elevated layers. Where they differ is where the brand shows up and how much contrast they ask for.
